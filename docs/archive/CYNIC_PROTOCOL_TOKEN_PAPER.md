Status: public doctrine draft.

CYNIC Protocol. It is not a MiCA crypto-asset white paper, not legal advice, not
an offer to the public, and not an admission-to-trading document.

## 1. Canonical Separation

```text
TALARIA SAS = legal genesis entity.
CYNIC Framework = technical stack, Kernel, judge, agents and data systems.
CYNIC Protocol = on-chain and off-chain coordination layer.
$CYNIC = protocol, access, compute, reputation and memetic asset.
```

`$CYNIC` is not the TALARIA SAS token. It does not represent shares, legal
dividends, debt, a claim on company assets, a claim on IP, or a right to control
the legal organs of TALARIA SAS.

The token can participate in protocol coordination only where rules are public,
auditable and distinct from corporate governance.

---

## 2. Purpose

`$CYNIC` exists to coordinate a network that judges, routes, rewards and
protects digital activity through proof, reputation and measured contribution.

The token may be used for:

- access to protocol services;
- payment or prioritisation of compute;
- operator staking and possible slashing;
- rewards for verified contribution;
- liquidity or security incentives where risk is public;
- protocol governance over non-legal parameters;
- E-Score weighting where balance is not the sole signal;
- public burn or buyback mechanisms after costs, taxes and reserves.

The token must not be marketed as passive exposure to TALARIA SAS or as a right
to corporate revenue.

---

## 3. Network Functions

### 3.1 Access

`$CYNIC` may unlock or meter access to protocol services such as judgment APIs,
agent routes, scoring systems, operator registries, proofs or compute queues.

Access rules must be documented before enforcement.

### 3.2 Compute

`$CYNIC` may be used to pay, reserve or prioritise inference, RPC, storage,
indexing, monitoring, routing or other network resources.

Compute pricing should be tied to actual cost and scarcity, not to arbitrary
promises of token appreciation.

### 3.3 Operator Staking

Operators may stake `$CYNIC` to signal accountability for service quality,
uptime, data integrity, security, routing, execution or liquidity provision.

Any slashing rule must be public before stake is accepted.

### 3.4 E-Score

The E-Score measures verified responsibility in the network. Holding many tokens
must not be sufficient to obtain high authority.

E-Score can combine:

- contribution history;
- service reliability;
- risk taken;
- useful signal;
- public proof of work;
- incident response;
- token commitment where relevant.

### 3.5 Rewards

Protocol rewards may compensate measured contribution. They are not dividends.

Eligible contributions can include:

- compute served;
- verified integrations;
- useful data;
- liquidity risk;
- audits;
- bug reports;
- uptime;
- ecosystem work;
- market-making under public rules;
- security response.

No contribution, no protocol reward.

## 4. Revenue Boundary

Revenue must be separated by source and owner:

| Flow | Owner / Source | Treatment |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Corporate revenue | TALARIA SAS contracts, invoices, IP | taxable corporate revenue |
| Protocol fees | smart contracts or protocol treasuries | routed by published waterfall |
| Operator payments | service agreements or protocol rules | payment for measured service |
| Token burns | smart contract or treasury policy | no promised yield |
| Buybacks | discretionary or rule-based surplus use | after costs, taxes and reserves |

The protocol waterfall is defined in
`docs/foundation/CYNIC_PROTOCOL_REVENUE_WATERFALL.md`.

Core rule:

```text
Costs before surplus.
Tax before rewards.
Survival before burn.
Contribution before multiplier.
No passive revenue by default.
```

## 5. Launch Doctrine

`$CYNIC` must not launch as a tradable asset until the following are true:

1. The token/equity firewall is public and internally accepted.
2. TALARIA SAS legal genesis path is clear, or an explicit legal alternative is
   documented.
3. The mint, authorities, multisigs, supply and treasury wallets are documented.
4. Any insider, team, operator, liquidity or market-making allocation is public.
5. No private party has hidden launch timing or hidden allocation.
6. The communication pack avoids equity, dividend, revenue-share and guaranteed
   buyback language.
7. A lawyer has reviewed the intended public launch route.
8. If required, the MiCA white paper and notification process are prepared
   before public offering or admission to trading in the EU.

`$CYNIC` can be discussed before launch as a future protocol asset, but not sold
or promoted as a tradable opportunity before the launch doctrine is satisfied.

Buyback or burn, if any, occurs only after costs, taxes, reserves and security.

## 7. Open Legal Questions

These questions must be answered before launch:

1. Is the intended launch an offer to the public in the EU?
2. Is admission to trading being sought by the issuer, a trading platform, or a
   third party?
3. Does the token qualify as a crypto-asset under MiCA Title II, or could it be
   excluded, an e-money token, an asset-referenced token, or a financial
   instrument?
4. Which entity is the offeror or person seeking admission to trading?
5. Which jurisdiction is the home Member State?
6. Is an AMF notification required, and when?
7. What marketing communications require review before publication?
8. How are taxes, accounting and crypto asset valuation handled?
9. What AML/CFT obligations arise from the distribution path?
10. What disclaimers and risk warnings must be published?

- it has been reviewed for MiCA and French law implications;
