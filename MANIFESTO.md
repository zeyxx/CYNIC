# CYNIC Manifesto

Version: 0.1  
Status: public doctrine draft  
Runtime authority: none

This manifesto preserves direction. It does not prove implementation, grant
authority, define financial rights, or replace CYNIC's machine-readable
contracts.

## 1. The Problem

A person now operates across source repositories, machines, agents, models,
clouds, networks, wallets, communities, and markets. These systems retain
fragments of the person's work, identity, context, and authority, but rarely
expose a coherent account of:

- what was intended;
- what was observed;
- who or what acted;
- which authority allowed the action;
- what matter changed;
- what outcome followed;
- what remains unknown.

More automation without a membrane increases opacity and dependence.

## 2. The Human Is the Sovereign Root

CYNIC begins from one invariant:

> The human remains the sovereign authority over their private organism.

Agents, companies, protocols, models, markets, and applications may receive
bounded authority. None may silently become the source of the person's intent
or amplify its own powers.

Sovereignty is not isolation. It is the ability to connect systems while
retaining legible authority, consent, provenance, and exit.

## 3. CYNIC Is the Membrane

CYNIC connects planes without pretending they are one system:

```text
human intent
  -> private runtime and data
  -> agents and inference
  -> source, build, artifact, and deployment
  -> hosts, fleets, and federations
  -> selected public evidence
  -> Solana commitments and settlement
  -> Futard.io and futarchic audiences
```

The membrane observes, filters, translates, bounds, and proves exchanges
between these planes. It does not erase their independent authorities.

At every scale CYNIC seeks the same shape:

```text
desired state
  -> observe
  -> reconcile
  -> bounded action
  -> read-back proof
```

Intent flows downward. Evidence flows upward. Unknown remains data.

## 4. Coordination Without Capture

CYNIC coordinates humans and agents without requiring one omnipotent global
agent. Coordination may be advisory, negotiated, delegated, or market-informed.
Enforcement exists only at declared capability and mutation boundaries.

Every delegated workload must retain:

- its principal and parent;
- its scope and budgets;
- the evidence it consumed;
- the authority it received;
- the actions it attempted;
- its outcome and residual unknowns.

Chat history, model memory, and provider sessions are working context, not
authority.

## 5. Talaria Writes Code

Talaria is the legal and productive genesis operator that writes and proposes
code for CYNIC. It does not bypass the membrane it develops.

A Talaria-produced mutation should travel through:

```text
hypothesis
  -> isolated workspace
  -> attributed source change
  -> reproducible build
  -> conformance
  -> explicit admission
  -> bounded rollout
  -> measured outcome
  -> promotion or rollback
```

This is the beginning of governed self-evolution: the organism can improve its
own matter without confusing authorship, execution, evidence, and admission.

## 6. Intelligence Must Remain Sovereign

CYNIC Askesis is intended to become a durable digital companion: contextual,
helpful, contestable, and subordinate to the user.

CYNIC Inference provides portable intelligence across local and remote models.
Models, engines, accelerators, and providers remain adapters. A private runtime
must continue to function when any one provider disappears.

CYNIC should let a non-expert use machine learning on their own data through
simple universal ports. Simplicity must not hide consent, provenance, model
limits, data quality, cost, or uncertainty.

Rust-native primitives are built where ownership, safety, portability, or
observability justifies them. Existing systems are integrated behind ports when
rewriting them would add complexity without sovereignty.

## 7. Proof Before Publicity

Private activity is not public material by default.

```text
private event
  -> local evidence
  -> classification
  -> disclosure proposal
  -> explicit consent or pre-authorized policy
  -> minimization
  -> commitment or publication
  -> publication proof
```

Depending on the policy, the public may receive a complete event, a redacted
attestation, an aggregate metric, a cryptographic commitment, or nothing.

Transparency without consent becomes surveillance. Privacy without provenance
becomes unverifiable assertion. CYNIC must preserve both.

## 8. Solana, Futard.io, and Futarchy

Solana is CYNIC's strategic public commitment and settlement plane. It does not
replace local observation or convert an unsupported claim into a fact.

Futard.io projects explicitly disclosed matter to a futarchic audience. Markets
estimate consequences under declared resolution criteria. They do not create
factual truth.

The intended loop is:

```text
thesis
  -> measurable expected outcome
  -> public commitment or market
  -> bounded execution
  -> post-decision evidence
  -> resolution
  -> reputation and policy update
```

One-person futarchy means that a person can expose selected decisions, work,
predictions, and outcomes to an audience without surrendering the private
organism that produced them.

## 9. Reputation Is an Interpretation

E-Score may interpret attributed evidence through community-specific,
versioned registries. It must never claim to measure universal human worth.

A defensible reputation result names:

- the registry and version;
- the evidence and provenance used;
- its rules and uncertainty;
- its intended use;
- its freshness and expiration;
- its route for review or contestation.

We reject invisible scoring, irreversible behavioral caste, and automatic
authority derived from wealth, sacrifice, popularity, or opaque inference.

## 10. Markets Are Not Reality

Markets can aggregate expectations. Companies can hold legal rights. Protocols
can execute deterministic rules. Agents can propose actions. Models can infer.
None of these alone establishes reality.

Reality enters the organism through attributable observation and survives as
inspectable evidence. Judgment remains explicit, versioned, and contestable.

## 11. The Standard We Accept

CYNIC is not complete because its architecture is broad or its vocabulary is
ambitious. A capability exists only when its vertical is executable:

```text
intent
  -> authority
  -> observation
  -> bounded mutation
  -> read-back
  -> durable proof
  -> recovery
```

We prefer one narrow, reproducible vertical over ten planned control planes.
We label absence and uncertainty rather than presenting aspiration as
convergence.

## 12. Direction

CYNIC aims to give one person the transparent, sovereign, agentic and economic
leverage previously reserved for large organizations, while retaining stricter
limits on authority than those organizations usually provide.

The organism is private first, publicly accountable by choice, intelligent
without provider capture, coordinated without an omnipotent controller, and
capable of evolving without erasing the history of its own matter.
