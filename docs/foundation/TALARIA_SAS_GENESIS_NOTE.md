# TALARIA SAS Genesis Note

Status: public doctrine draft.

This document defines why TALARIA SAS exists, what it owns, what it operates and
what it does not promise to `$CYNIC` holders. It is not legal advice and does not
replace statutes, shareholder agreements, tax advice or counsel review.

## 1. Role

```text
TALARIA SAS is the legal genesis entity operating and governing the CYNIC
framework/protocol.
```

TALARIA SAS exists to give the CYNIC ecosystem a clear legal trunk:

- company statutes;
- contracts;
- invoices;
- IP ownership and licensing;
- corporate wallets;
- tax treatment;
- accounting;
- employment or service agreements;
- institutional relationships;
- public accountability.

The company is not the protocol itself. It can operate, protect, license and
govern parts of the framework, but it must not collapse legal equity and
protocol tokens into one instrument.

---

## 2. Scope

TALARIA SAS may own or control:

- CYNIC marks, public policies and standards;
- software IP and licensing rights;
- official websites and public surfaces;
- corporate treasury and tax reserve wallets;
- client contracts;
- B2B, B2G or institutional relationships;
- operator agreements;
- IP or governance fees from branches using CYNIC standards;
- legal claims and obligations.

TALARIA SAS may not use `$CYNIC` to bypass:

- company law;
- shareholder rights;
- accounting;
- taxes;
- regulated financial activity;
- AML/CFT obligations;
- consumer protection;
- public disclosure duties where applicable.

---

## 3. Token Boundary

`$CYNIC` is not an action of TALARIA SAS.

Holding `$CYNIC` does not grant:

- shares;
- dividends;
- debt claims;
- legal voting rights in TALARIA SAS;
- rights to company assets;
- rights to IP;
- rights to company revenue;
- automatic rights to future subsidiaries;
- control over the president, statutes or legal governance.

TALARIA SAS shareholders obtain their rights from statutes, shareholder
agreements and French law. `$CYNIC` holders obtain only the rights explicitly
published in protocol rules and smart contracts.

---

## 4. Public Corporate Commitments

TALARIA SAS should publicly commit to:

```text
Token is not equity.
Equity is not protocol access.
Protocol rewards are not dividends.
Buyback is not yield.
Governance protocol is not legal control.
```

Operational commitments:

- no hidden token allocation for insiders;
- no hidden launch timing;
- no undisclosed market-making power;
- no promise of legal dividends to token holders;
- no promise of token buyback frequency, floor or amount;
- no commingling of personal, corporate and protocol wallets;
- no burn before costs, taxes, reserves and security needs.

## 6. Wallet Doctrine

Every wallet must have an owner, purpose and movement policy.

Minimum registry:

| Wallet | Owner | Function |
|--------|-------|----------|
| SAS Treasury | TALARIA SAS | corporate revenue, costs, taxes, accounting |
| Tax Reserve | TALARIA SAS | fiat/stable reserve for fiscal obligations |
| Protocol Treasury | smart contract or multisig | protocol fees and public allocations |
| Burn Wallet | smart contract or public address | verifiable burns |
| Operator Payments | TALARIA SAS or branch | measured service payments |

Rule:

```text
No critical entity wallet should be a personal wallet.
No movement without purpose.
No public treasury promise without accounting clarity.
```

## 7. Relationship With Percolator Or Perp Markets

A perp market on `$CYNIC` can create strong public interpretation risk. Before
any request, endorsement or coordination with a perp venue, TALARIA SAS and the
protocol team should decide:

- whether `$CYNIC` is already launched or only planned;
- whether the request is made by TALARIA SAS, the protocol, the community or a
  third party;
- whether the market creates admission-to-trading implications;
- whether marketing around fees, buyback or burn needs counsel review;
- whether public risk warnings are available;
- whether market-making and liquidity support are disclosed.

Phrase to prefer:

```text
Market structure must follow published protocol rules and legal review.
```

Phrase to avoid:

```text
Perp fees will pump or reward holders.
```
