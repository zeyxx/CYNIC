## 1. Problem

The `$ASDFASDFA` cult weakens when all available attention goes into building
CYNIC, Talaria and adjacent infrastructure.

This is not a technical failure. It is an allocation failure:

```text
Build work creates substance.
Bagwork creates distribution.
Futarchy converts distribution into market coordination.
```

Without bagwork, CYNIC becomes a private machine with no memetic surface.
Without CYNIC, `$ASDFASDFA` becomes empty posting. The operating rule is to keep
both loops alive without confusing their legal or economic roles.

## 2. Separation Of Roles

| Surface | Role | Public function | Hard boundary |
|---------|------|-----------------|---------------|
| `$ASDFASDFA` | Existing memecoin community asset | Cult, distribution, experiments, Percolator candidate | No claim on Talaria SAS equity or CYNIC protocol revenues |
| Talaria SAS | Legal genesis entity | Contracts, IP, corporate surface, institutional trust | Not a memecoin and not owned by token holders |
| CYNIC | Technical/protocol framework | Judgment engine, proof, agents, kernel, economic rules | Not a vague hype wrapper |
| Futarchy / MetaDAO | Market decision rail | Lets markets price proposals and coordinate attention | Market price is signal, not truth |
| Twitter/X | Distribution arena | Narrative, recruitment, feedback, public proof-of-work | Not canonical memory or governance |

Canonical public phrasing:

```text
$ASDFASDFA is the cult distribution shell.
Talaria is the legal/public vehicle.
CYNIC is the judgment engine.
Futarchy is the market coordination rail.
```

Forbidden compression:

```text
$ASDFASDFA holders own Talaria.
$ASDFASDFA guarantees exposure to CYNIC revenue.
Talaria/CYNIC will pump ASDFASDFA.
Futarchy guarantees the correct outcome.
```

## 3. Strategic Thesis

`$ASDFASDFA` should not be positioned as "the company token" or "the CYNIC
token". Its cleanest role is:

```text
The living cult and adversarial test community around CYNIC-grade judgment.
```

That gives it a legitimate job:

1. stress-test narratives;
2. recruit high-agency contributors;
3. provide memetic liquidity for experiments;
4. generate public data for CYNIC/Talaria judgment products;
5. serve as a cleaner market experiment candidate than an unlaunched `$CYNIC`.

The link to Talaria/CYNIC is therefore operational and cultural first, not
financial entitlement first.

## 5. Content Pillars

### 5.1 Cult Energy

Purpose: keep the tribe emotionally alive.

Examples:

- absurdist `$ASDFASDFA` conviction posts;
- "still building, still judging, still not explaining too much";
- rivalry posts against other cult tokens;
- lore fragments that do not require product comprehension.

Rule: cult posts should be short, repeatable and easy to quote.

### 5.2 Proof Of Work

Purpose: show that the cult is attached to an actual machine.

Examples:

- screenshots of CYNIC health, verdicts, tests, operators or agents;
- short clips of `/judge`, Talaria UI, X monitor, or Hermes signals;
- "what changed this week" build proof;
- one-liner linking a commit, artifact or public demo.

Rule: proof posts should be concrete. No abstract "soon" unless paired with a
visible artifact.

### 5.3 Futarchy / Market Coordination

Purpose: educate the audience that the cult is becoming a decision market, not
just a chat room.

Examples:

- explaining why market signals are useful but not sacred;
- tracking proposal readiness;
- comparing `$ASDFASDFA` as a live community asset versus `$CYNIC` as planned
  protocol fuel;
- public questions that can become prediction-market or futarchy proposals.

Rule: never present future market outcomes as guarantees.

### 5.4 Anti-Bullshit Judgment

Purpose: make CYNIC's core taste legible to the cult.

Examples:

- "CYNIC does not promise certainty; it prices doubt";
- token/rug judgment snippets;
- market hype autopsies;
- short EPOCHÉ explanations when evidence is insufficient.

Rule: this pillar can mention CYNIC directly because it is about the product's
actual judgment function.

## 6. Daily Bagwork Loop

Daily 30-minute floor:

1. **Scan:** check notifications, mentions, MetaDAO/Solana/futarchy accounts,
   and `$ASDFASDFA` adjacent chatter.
2. **Reply:** answer or quote 3-5 relevant posts. Prefer public replies over
   private thinking.
3. **Post:** publish one item from the content pillars.
4. **Recruit:** DM or tag one high-signal person only when there is a concrete
   reason.
5. **Capture:** log material discoveries through `cynic_observe` or a Kernel
   task, not a local scratch backlog.

The loop is intentionally small. Its goal is continuity, not a heroic campaign.

## 8. Futarchy Interface

Candidate questions should be written as market-resolvable decisions, not vague
aspirations.

Good:

```text
Should Talaria route the next public demo through a MetaDAO-style market before
shipping it?
```

```text
Should `$ASDFASDFA` be used as the first public Percolator/cult-screener
case study before any `$CYNIC` launch?
```

```text
Should the next visible milestone be X distribution, API integration, or
on-chain proof?
```

Bad:

```text
Will we make it?
```

```text
Is ASDFASDFA the future?
```

```text
Will this pump?
```

Futarchy is useful when the decision changes behavior. If the answer would not
change execution, do not marketize it.

## 9. Public Futarchy Interface

The public interface should make the work legible before asking anyone to care.
The community does not need raw internal complexity. It needs a visible decision
surface:

```text
What is being built?
Why does it matter to the cult?
What decision is open?
What evidence exists?
What happens if the market/community chooses A or B?
```

Minimum public surface:

1. **Manifest page:** one canonical page explaining ASDFASDFA -> Talaria ->
   CYNIC -> futarchy in plain language.
2. **Build ledger:** weekly public proof that long engineering hours are being
   converted into artifacts.
3. **Decision board:** current market/proposal questions, status and evidence.
4. **Outcome log:** what was decided, what was shipped, what was falsified.

This can start as a static page before becoming a full product. The first useful
version can be:

```text
/manifest
/build-log
/decisions
/outcomes
```

Public UX rule:

```text
The interface must show work before asking for belief.
```

If the cult sees long build hours attached to concrete outputs, the narrative
changes from "the founder disappeared" to "the machine is being forged".

## 11. Build-In-Public Proof Stream

Long development hours are distribution material if they are compressed into
proof.

Do not post raw exhaustion. Post visible deltas:

```json
{
  "schema_version": "build-proof-v1",
  "period": "2026-W27",
  "hours_spent": 18,
  "artifact": "CYNIC coordination/futarchy contract",
  "evidence": [
    "document created",
    "kernel observation logged",
    "next public interface gap identified"
  ],
  "why_cult_should_care": "This turns ASDFASDFA from passive meme into the public pressure layer around CYNIC/Talaria decisions."
}
```

Good public formats:

- weekly screenshot of shipped artifact plus one-line meaning;
- short "what changed while I was quiet" thread;
- build-log card with hours, artifact and next decision;
- before/after diff of public interface or kernel capability;
- "CYNIC gap found" post that explains what automation unlocks.

Bad public formats:

- vague grindset posting;
- huge changelogs without why;
- private sacrifice framed as entitlement;
- saying the community owes attention because work happened.

Rule:

```text
Every visible build proof must answer: what can the cult do with this now?
```

## 12. CYNIC Automation Gaps For Bagwork

CYNIC can reduce manual bagwork, but only if it treats distribution as a first
class sensing/action loop.

Current useful gaps:

| Gap | Needed capability | Bagwork unlocked |
|-----|-------------------|------------------|
| Public build ledger | Convert git commits, docs and observations into weekly public proof cards | "I was building this" posts |
| X opportunity scorer | Rank replies, accounts and topics by relevance to ASDFASDFA/Talaria/CYNIC | Better reply sessions |
| Manifest publisher | Render the current doctrine into a public static page | Shared canonical link |
| Futarchy decision board | Track proposed questions, evidence, status and outcomes | Market coordination surface |
| Narrative distiller | Turn dense technical work into 3 tweet-sized variants | Lower posting friction |
| Cult pulse monitor | Track mentions, replies, sentiment and silence windows | Detect weakening earlier |
| Bagwork task actuator | Create weekly tasks when public activity falls below floor | Anti-forgetting |

The highest-leverage first automation is:

```text
git/docs/kernel observations -> weekly build proof -> suggested posts -> public manifest/update
```

This is not a social-media bot first. It is a proof compressor first.

Implementation direction:

1. read local git diff, selected docs and `cynic_observe` events;
2. summarize into structured `build-proof-v1`;
3. generate 3 post candidates: cult, proof-of-work, futarchy;
4. let the human approve/edit before posting;
5. log what was published and what reactions came back.

Autonomous posting is a later step. The immediate win is removing the blank-page
cost and making build work visible.

## 13. Legal And Economic Guardrails

Use the fair-launch and token/equity firewall doctrine as hard constraints.

Allowed:

- `$ASDFASDFA` as a live cult and distribution shell;
- `$ASDFASDFA` as a community experiment around CYNIC-grade judgment;
- `$ASDFASDFA` as a cleaner existing-asset candidate for Percolator-style
  market experiments;
- Talaria/CYNIC build proofs shown to the cult;
- public uncertainty about future protocol token design.

Forbidden:

- saying `$ASDFASDFA` owns Talaria/CYNIC;
- promising buybacks, burns, floors, listings or revenue routing;
- implying insider timing;
- implying a `$CYNIC` launch before the public mechanics are documented;
- using corporate assets or protocol claims to manufacture false entitlement
  for an existing memecoin.

Practical sentence:

```text
$ASDFASDFA is where the cult lives today; CYNIC is the judgment machine being
built in public; Talaria is the legal vehicle protecting the work.
```

## 16. Definition Of Done

This operating contract is working when:

- `$ASDFASDFA` has visible weekly pulse;
- public posts can explain the relationship to Talaria/CYNIC in one sentence;
- futarchy questions become concrete decisions, not vibes;
- the public interface shows manifest, build proofs, decisions and outcomes;
- the cult receives proof-of-work often enough to stay attached to reality;
- CYNIC compresses engineering activity into usable distribution assets;
- CYNIC development no longer cannibalizes all distribution work;
- no communication breaks the token/equity firewall.
