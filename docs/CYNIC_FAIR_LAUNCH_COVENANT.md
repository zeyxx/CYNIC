# CYNIC Fair Launch Covenant

Status: public doctrine draft.

This covenant defines the minimum fairness rules before any public launch,
sale, liquidity event, exchange listing, perp market request or admission to
trading involving `$CYNIC`.

It is not legal advice, not a token sale document and not a MiCA white paper.

Related documents:

- `docs/foundation/CYNIC_PROTOCOL_TOKEN_PAPER.md`
- `docs/foundation/CYNIC_TOKEN_EQUITY_FIREWALL.md`
- `docs/foundation/TALARIA_SAS_GENESIS_NOTE.md`
- `docs/foundation/CYNIC_PROTOCOL_REVENUE_WATERFALL.md`

## 1. Core Law

```text
No hidden allocation.
No hidden timing.
No hidden power.
No hidden promise.
No private truth different from public truth.
```

The public must not discover after launch that insiders had a materially better
version of the rules.

---

## 2. Scope

This covenant applies to:

- `$CYNIC` mint or deployment;
- public sale;
- airdrop;
- liquidity pool creation;
- market-making program;
- CEX or DEX listing coordination;
- perp market request;
- operator allocation;
- team allocation;
- treasury allocation;
- community rewards;
- buyback or burn program;
- any public communication that can affect token expectations.

---

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Launch Disclosures

Before launch, publish:

1. token mint address or deployment procedure;
2. total supply or supply mechanics;
3. mint authority status;
4. freeze authority status;
5. upgrade authority status where relevant;
6. treasury wallets;
7. team, advisor, operator, investor and community allocations;
8. vesting, lockup and unlock rules;
9. liquidity plan;
10. market-making plan if any;
11. protocol fee routing;
12. burn or buyback policy if any;
13. legal and risk disclaimers;
14. official communication channels;
15. list of things explicitly not promised.

If a field is not final, it must be marked pending, not implied.

---

## 4. Insider Rules

Insiders include founders, employees, contractors, advisors, operators, market
makers, liquidity partners, investors, contributors with private information and
any wallet controlled by them.

Insiders must not receive:

- undisclosed allocations;
- undisclosed timing;
- undisclosed market-making rights;
- private promises of buyback or floor;
- private guarantees of listing support;
- private claims that contradict public documents.

Team and operator allocations are allowed only when public and time-locked,
performance-linked, contribution-linked or otherwise justified by explicit
rules.

---

## 5. Communication Rules

Allowed:

```text
$CYNIC is planned as the protocol asset of CYNIC Protocol.
$CYNIC is not equity in TALARIA SAS.
Protocol rewards require verified contribution.
Token mechanics will be published before launch.
Any buyback or burn happens only after costs, taxes, reserves and security.
```

Forbidden:

```text
Guaranteed yield.
Guaranteed buyback.
Guaranteed floor.
Guaranteed listing.
Guaranteed perp market.
Holders receive TALARIA SAS profits.
The token is backed by company assets.
Private early access for selected friends.
```

Perp-specific forbidden language:

```text
Perp fees will pump the token.
Perp market equals official endorsement.
Market launch guarantees burn or buyback.
```

---

## 6. Market Structure Rules

Any request for a `$CYNIC` perp market or exchange listing must answer:

1. Who is making the request?
2. Is the requester TALARIA SAS, the protocol team, a community member or a
   third party?
3. Is the token already launched?
4. Are token mechanics public?
5. Is there reliable spot liquidity and pricing?
6. Is there an index methodology?
7. Are fees, buyback, burn and treasury routes documented?
8. Has counsel reviewed whether the request changes regulatory obligations?
9. Are market-making arrangements disclosed?
10. Are risk warnings public?

Until these are answered, `$ASDFASDFA` is the cleaner Percolator candidate
because it is already an existing memecoin community asset rather than the
planned protocol asset of TALARIA SAS / CYNIC Protocol.

---

## 7. Regulatory Readiness

Before an EU public offering or admission to trading is pursued, the team must
complete a legal route memo covering:

- MiCA classification;
- whether a white paper is required;
- whether AMF notification is required;
- home Member State;
- offeror or person seeking admission to trading;
- marketing communications;
- risk warnings;
- AML/CFT exposure;
- tax and accounting treatment;
- DASP/CASP implications if services are provided.

The public token paper must not pretend to be a MiCA white paper unless counsel
has explicitly prepared it as such.

---

## 8. Definition Of Done

Launch fairness is satisfied only when:

- public rules match private reality;
- token/equity firewall is public;
- wallets and authorities are documented;
- allocations and lockups are public;
- legal route is documented;
- no insider has undisclosed informational advantage;
- no communication promises yield, equity, dividends, floor or guaranteed
  buyback;
- all launch materials are consistent with TALARIA SAS genesis documents.
