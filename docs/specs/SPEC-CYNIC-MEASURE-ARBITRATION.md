## 1. Context and Problem Statement

`SPEC-CYNIC-OWNERSHIP.md` established that governance weight R-C is derived from observed
contribution and is never stored. That leaves a harder question open: **which observation,
and for what.**

The existing corpus answers it eleven times and inconsistently. `CYNIC_E_SCORE.md` scores
seven abstract dimensions. `CYNIC_PROTOCOL_TOKEN_REVENUE_WATERFALL.md:277` makes it a vote
weight. `CYNIC_ORGANISM_POLICY_MATRIX.md:19-26` demands six fields per rule and no rule in
`contracts/` carries them. The repository's own mathematics audit refuted the resulting
constants — 0.618 as *structurally aesthetic choices*, and the anti-whale modifier which
multiplied the stake ratio from 100 to 1 000 000 (`docs/archive/reports/2026-09-02-mathematics-geometry-audit.md:183-192`).

The failure mode is not bad arithmetic. It is **one number asked to answer several questions**.
A single scalar mixing capital, contribution, and tenure is asked "who deserves authority",
"how much infrastructure to buy", and "how long this actor has been present". It answers
none of them, and it answers all of them in a way that favours incumbents.

Two further defects, both observed rather than assumed:

**Aggregation is not alignment.** A headcount of contributors is systematically
miscalibrated. Every agent wants more compute; none wants to cap it. The majority preference
is inflationary while system survival requires deflation. Counting heads does not align
anyone — it only measures preference, which is what a market price does, and which is
circular here because the token is denominated in a supply-unconstrained top-down currency.

**Abstraction does not travel.** The 7-dimension E-Score is claimed to reward *"verified
contribution to shared digital goods"*, but GitHub Stars are a quantity anyone can purchase.
The property is not false; it does not survive contact with the thing it names.

## 2. Decision and Formal Specification

Authority is not allocated by count and not by a composite. It is **allocated by which
measurement each decision needs**, and measurements fail differently, so mixing them
destroys the signal.

### 2.1 The Allocation

| Power | Measure | Class | Why this measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P-infra** — infrastructure budget | observed consumption: cpu_ms, ram_bytes·s, vram_bytes, joules, wall_ms | `sensor` | Physical. Measured, not declared. Needs no consensus. |
| **P-next** — what to build next | attention flow over a decaying window | `revealed` | Demand, not stated intent. |
| **P-legit** — legitimacy and continuity | attention stock (cumulative retention) | `revealed` | Durability, not novelty. |
| **P-admit** — admission and contribution | third-party consumption of a produced artefact | `revealed` | Preference revealed by use. |
| **P-treasury** — treasury allocation | capped envelope | `capped` | Where confiscation risk lives. Weighted scoring is forbidden here. |

### 2.2 Formal Invariants

1. **INV-08 (One measure, one power)**: A measurement may govern at most one power. No
   measure appears in two rows.
2. **INV-09 (No beneficiary measures itself)**: No power may be governed by a measurement its
   beneficiary can influence directly. Stated preference and self-reported quantity are
   inadmissible for every power.
3. **INV-10 (Flow and stock are never commensurable)**: For any quantity `q`, `q_flow` and
   `q_stock` must govern different powers. An actor may never convert age into authority.
4. **INV-11 (Treasury is capped, never weighted)**: P-treasury is allocated by envelope and
   cap, never by a score. No measurement contributes to a treasury decision.
5. **INV-12 (Retention is relative)**: Retention is a share of consumers over a window,
   divided by consumers over lifetime. An absolute count is inadmissible, because retention
   is necessarily below one: a service used by everyone is used by nobody attentively.
6. **INV-13 (Contribution without consumption scores zero)**: Work with no observable
   third-party consumption has weight zero. Activity is not value.
7. **INV-14 (No conservation law for attention)**: Attention is generated by the act of
   paying it and destroyed by neglect. No rule may derive from an assumed conservation of
   attention.
8. **INV-15 (Losses are modelled, not assumed absent)**: A weight function with no
   loss term is inadmissible. Capture-without-retention, context rivalry, verification
   cost, attribution, and homogenisation must each appear.
9. **INV-16 (No constant without calibration and a falsifier)**: A measurement parameter is
   `null` until calibrated against a measured corpus. An aesthetic constant is inadmissible,
   including by analogy.
10. **INV-17 (The arbiter is itself bounded)**: Whoever arbitrates between measures is
    subject to the published, measurable, contestable constraint set, and may be removed
    without damage to the system's existence.

### 2.3 The Anti-Capture Argument

`INV-09` is the load-bearing one, and it is symmetric in a way that is easy to get wrong.

It is not enough to say "nobody measures their own contribution". GitHub Stars are not
self-reported *by the buyer* — the buyer buys a measurement that someone else then uses.
That is still capture, one remove out. The check that survives is **third-party
consumption**: whether *this* actor uses the artefact. That is not a score anyone can
manufacture, because manufacture requires producing something other actors actually need.

This is why a maintainer of a service used by 2% of the community outranks a holder of 1%
who does nothing, and why "0.1% supply" is the wrong unit of merit in the first place:
the unit is consumption, and supply is not a denominator for anything.

### 2.4 The Loss Terms

Five leaks, of which two are invisible to systems that only measure their own activity:

| Leak | Mechanism | Who pays it |
|---|---|---|
| **capture without retention** | peak, then nothing | content, overwhelmingly |
| **context rivalry** | *N* parallel agents destroy each other's context | agent swarms specifically |
| **verification** | attention spent coordinating instead of producing | any proof-bearing system |
| **attribution** | attention flows to the agent rather than the work | reputation-governed systems |
| **homogenisation** | universal use destroys discrimination | this specification's own metric |

The second is the load-bearing one for a runtime whose product is agent orchestration. A
swarm buys parallelism by destroying context, and that cost appears in no agent's self-report.
`EnergyConsumed` without `workload_advanced` is its measure.

### 2.5 Why Not Attention as Energy

The heuristic is useful for generating hypotheses and inadmissible as justification, for two
reasons that are structural rather than stylistic.

**There is no conservation law.** Electricity conserves; attention is created by being spent
and destroyed by neglect. An energy metaphor implies convertibility with no waste, and a
model with no waste term generates metrics that mean nothing (`INV-08` in spirit,
`INV-14` formally).

**`V × I = P` computes energy, not power.** Intensity of contribution is a flow; accumulated
volume is a stock. Their product is not a rate. The power is the time derivative. This is the
same stock-versus-flow confusion as `INV-10`, arriving from physics rather than governance,
and it is why a cumulative metric produces a gerontocracy.

The defensible model is contagion: a quantity that propagates by contact, saturates, and
decays. Saturation is what prevents unbounded labour converting into unbounded authority and
is what keeps `INV-13` meaningful.

### 2.6 Seniority

Tenure is a *penalty on defection*, never a vote. It belongs to `R-D` in
`contracts/ownership.json`, not to `R-C`. Conflating them produces a gerontocracy, which is
the same error as mixing flow and stock in `INV-10`.

### 2.7 Alignment Is a Solvency Condition

Not consensus, and not a majority. **No actor may gain more by the system dying than by the
system living.** An unconstrained treasury is the failure: one number is simultaneously the
bet and the defence, so a legal action consumes the whole system. This is why
`P-treasury` is capped rather than scored, and why `contracts/ownership.json →
treasury_constraint` forbids a treasury being the sole legal defence of a permissionless
system.

### 2.8 The Residual Problem

Arbitration costs something, and in a single-operator system the arbiter is the operator.
This reintroduces exactly the centralisation the design exists to prevent, and no mechanism
removes it. `INV-17` states the residual honestly rather than solving it: the arbiter is
itself constrained, published, and removable without destroying the system. It is a design
constraint on a person, not a proof.

## 3. Top-Down Causal Chain Impact

- **Constituent / Operator Intent**: arbitrates measure selection; bound by `INV-17`.
- **Protocol Types & Contracts**: `Power` and `MeasureClass` as distinct enums, so that
  `INV-08` is representable rather than merely asserted. `contracts/measure-arbitration.json`
  holds the allocation table.
- **Execution & Sandboxing Boundary**: a measurement must be consumed through admission like
  any other input. A score that reaches a decision without passing `admit_agent_workload` is
  not a measurement, it is an assertion.
- **Read-Back Proof & Observation**: sensor-class measures come from the host observation
  layer. `cynic runtime capacity --json` emits `CapacityAssessmentV1` with
  `residual_unknowns`, which is the correct shape. A measurement with unknowns reports
  `unknown`, never zero.

## 4. Blast Radius and Non-Goals

- **In-Scope**: the allocation table, `INV-08..17`, the loss terms, the anti-capture
  argument, and the checkable properties.
- **Explicit Non-Goals**:
  - Calibration. `lambda` and `saturation` stay `null`.
  - Any economic instrument. `P-treasury` is allocated, not earned.
  - Any sociological claim about communities. The 7-dimension E-Score is not replaced here;
    it is declared non-load-bearing in `contracts/claims.json` (`CLM-0018`, `falsified`).
- **Forbidden Interactions**:
  - One measure for two powers.
  - A power whose measure its beneficiary can move.
  - `q_flow` and `q_stock` governing the same power.
  - A score in any treasury decision.
  - A non-null calibration constant without a measured corpus.

## 5. Verification and Property Tests

| ID | Property | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| PROP-05 | Each measure governs at most one power | `measure_arbitration_allocates_one_measure_per_power` |
| PROP-06 | No power uses a self-reported or stated-preference measure | `no_power_is_governed_by_a_self_reported_measure` |
| PROP-07 | `q_flow` and `q_stock` govern different powers | `flow_and_stock_never_govern_the_same_power` |
| PROP-08 | No score reaches a treasury decision | `treasury_is_capped_and_never_weighted` |
| PROP-09 | Calibration constants are `null` | `no_calibrated_constant_without_a_measured_corpus` |
| PROP-10 | Every load-bearing power names a falsifier | `every_measure_names_a_falsifier` |

**Read-back probe.** A measurement entering a decision must satisfy the same
`RuntimeProofBundle` conditions as any other input, and its unknowns must reach
`residual_unknowns` rather than being clamped to a passing value.

## 6. Migration and Rollout Plan

1. Land `contracts/measure-arbitration.json` with all six properties executable. No runtime
   change.
2. Add `Power` and `MeasureClass` to `cynic-protocol` such that `INV-08` is unrepresentable.
3. Wire the retention signal: `ChatTrace` token counts are `Option<u64> = None`
   (`crates/cynic-inference/src/domain/chat_trace.rs:26`) because no production `TraceSink`
   is attached at the four CLI call sites. Until then `P-next` and `P-admit` derive to
   `unknown`, which is the honest state and not zero.
4. Declare the sixteen constitutional boundary in `contracts/foundation.json`.
5. Leave `lambda` and `saturation` `null`.

## 7. What Would Falsify This Spec

| | Falsifier |
|---|---|
| INV-08 | A composite measure demonstrably serving two powers better than two separate measures. The anti-whale refutation is a counterexample for a *wrong* composite, not evidence for a good one. |
| INV-09 | A self-reported measure that resists capture in practice at community scale. |
| INV-13 | A system where unconsumed activity reliably predicts survival better than consumption does. |
| INV-14 | An attention model with predictive gain from an explicit conservation assumption. |
| INV-17 | A system where the arbiter's removal is materially cheaper than the arbitration is worth — i.e. where `INV-17` is pure cost. |
