## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this memo is to prepare a structured counsel review before any:

- public launch of `$CYNIC`;
- public sale;
- airdrop with economic expectations;
- DEX liquidity event;
- CEX listing request;
- perp market request;
- admission to trading;
- communication implying buyback, burn, rewards or protocol fees.

The memo should help decide whether a MiCA white paper, AMF notification or
other regulatory step is required.

## 3. Token Classification Questions

Counsel must determine whether `$CYNIC`, as designed, is:

1. a crypto-asset under MiCA Title II;
2. an asset-referenced token;
3. an e-money token;
4. a financial instrument;
5. outside one or more relevant regimes;
6. subject to additional national rules or consumer protection rules.

Facts to provide to counsel:

- intended token functions;
- supply mechanics;
- allocation table;
- vesting and lockups;
- launch path;
- treasury policy;
- burn and buyback policy;
- operator rewards;
- staking and slashing;
- governance rights;
- access rights;
- market structure plans;
- marketing materials.

## 4. Offer / Admission Questions

Before public launch or trading venue coordination:

1. Is there an offer to the public in the EU?
2. Who is the offeror?
3. Is a third party seeking admission to trading?
4. Is TALARIA SAS requesting admission to trading?
5. Is a DEX pool creation treated differently from a public offer?
6. Does a perp market request create admission-to-trading or marketing issues?
7. Is an AMF notification required?
8. Is a MiCA white paper required?
9. What timing applies before publication?
10. What marketing communications must be reviewed?

No communication should imply that these answers are already settled before
counsel review.

---

## 5. Token / Equity Firewall

`$CYNIC` must not grant:

- shares in TALARIA SAS;
- dividends;
- legal voting rights in TALARIA SAS;
- rights to company profits;
- rights to company assets;
- debt claims;
- control over directors, president or statutes;
- automatic claims on subsidiaries;
- automatic buybacks;
- guaranteed yield;
- guaranteed price floor.

Allowed only if documented and reviewed:

- access;
- compute payments;
- operator staking;
- slashing;
- protocol rewards for measured contribution;
- protocol governance over non-legal parameters;
- buyback or burn after costs, taxes, reserves and security needs.

## 6. Corporate Law Questions

Before TALARIA SAS genesis:

1. Who is president?
2. What is the registered office?
3. What is the initial capital?
4. Who are the initial shareholders?
5. Are Ragnar and Erin shareholders, token holders, contributors, symbolic
   founders, or outside the cap table?
6. What IP is assigned to TALARIA SAS?
7. What IP is licensed instead of assigned?
8. Which wallets are corporate, protocol, operator or personal?
9. Who can sign contracts?
10. Which decisions require shareholder approval?

These questions must not be resolved through token communications.

---

## 7. Tax And Accounting Questions

Counsel and accountant must review:

- corporate revenue in fiat, stablecoins and volatile tokens;
- token receipts;
- token payments;
- token treasury valuation;
- buyback accounting;
- burn treatment;
- VAT exposure;
- payroll or contractor payments in crypto;
- reserve policy;
- off-ramp process;
- reporting obligations.

Core doctrine:

```text
No burn before tax reserve.
No reward before accounting clarity.
No public treasury promise without fiscal review.
```

---

## 8. AML / CFT And Service Questions

Questions:

1. Does TALARIA SAS provide crypto-asset services?
2. Does any product create custody, exchange, transfer or execution exposure?
3. Are token payments accepted directly by TALARIA SAS?
4. Are users identifiable or pseudonymous?
5. Are operator rewards subject to screening?
6. Does a future DASP/CASP status become necessary?
7. Which jurisdictions create additional obligations?

This memo should not assume that all protocol activity is outside regulated
service scope.
